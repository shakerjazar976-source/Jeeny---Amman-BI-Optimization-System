# Abstract

To support prescriptive decision-making, the project developed an Amman Bottleneck Index (ABI). The ABI combines trip pressure, revenue pressure, duration pressure, distance pressure, risk pressure, and peak pressure into a score from 0 to 100. This score was calculated at pickup-zone, zone-hour, and OD-pair levels. The project also generated driver allocation recommendations based on demand pressure and bottleneck pressure. This allows the BI system to recommend how drivers should be distributed by zone and hour rather than only describing where trips happened in the past.

The project concludes that Jeeny should prioritize Amman, build an Airport-centered operational playbook, reposition drivers before peak hours, monitor high-risk segments, review weak OD corridors, and use Streamlit as a daily governorate monitoring layer. The final BI solution demonstrates how Power BI, Python, and Streamlit can work together to move the project from descriptive dashboards to predictive and prescriptive business intelligence.

As part of the prescriptive approach to making decisions, the Amman Bottleneck Index (ABI) index is created for use in making recommendations. The index takes the following factors into account: trip pressure, revenue pressure, duration pressure, distance pressure, risk pressure, and peak pressure, and then creates a score between 0 and 100. The ABI was created in terms of pickup zones, zone-hour analysis, and OD-pair analysis. Recommendations about driver allocations by zones and hours were made in relation to demand pressure and bottleneck pressure.

Concluding the project, we recommend that Jeeny give priority to Amman, create an Airport-based playbook, allocate drivers prior to peak hours, identify risks in segments, identify weaknesses in OD corridors, and implement Streamlit as a governorate-level monitoring tool. In conclusion, the BI solution presented above shows how to utilize Power BI, Python, and Streamlit as a part of the transition from descriptive dashboards to business intelligence.
